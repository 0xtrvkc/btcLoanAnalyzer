How the numbers connect

Your original BTC and the BTC bought with the loan keep separate cost bases. Unused loan cash stays as cash and is used first when you repay.

Debt at exit = principal × (1 + APR × days from loan date to exit ÷ 365)
Equity = all BTC × price + cash − debt at exit
P/L since borrowing = equity − your BTC × price when you borrowed
P/L from today = equity − today’s equity
Loan trade = loan BTC × (price − purchase price) − all interest

Below the liquidation price the open-position model stops: real proceeds depend on your lender. A later rebound does not undo an earlier liquidation.

Model assumptions & limits

Interest: simple interest, principal × APR × days ÷ 365. “Now” views use interest to today; target views use interest to your exit date. Fees, tax, slippage and compounding are excluded.

90-day probability: 2Φ[ln(barrier ÷ price) ÷ (σ√(90/365))], the reflection principle for a driftless log-price. The barrier uses the debt you will owe in 90 days. No jumps or volatility changes; not a forecast.

Cycle score: heuristic weights, not backtested probabilities. It is not a borrowing recommendation.
