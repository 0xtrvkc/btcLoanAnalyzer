Rows update live with your inputs above. Equity = BTC value − loan principal. Net P/L / Return use your Profit Calculator deploy % and target settings, valued against your collateral's current price (loan interest not deducted). P/L on Amount Borrowed isolates just the leveraged bet — the BTC bought with your loan (plus any loan cash still sitting undeployed) minus the loan itself. It excludes your original collateral entirely, so it needs no Cost Basis entry — just an active loan — and its breakeven is always the price you deployed the loan at (today's price by default, or set "Deployed BTC at price" in the Profit Calculator above if you drew the loan at a different, known price). P/L vs Cost Basis (whole balance) instead pretends your entire current BTC stack — original collateral plus whatever the loan bought — was acquired at your entry price; set your Cost Basis above to see it. It's the simpler, more optimistic number people often eyeball, but it overstates gains (or understates losses) versus what you actually put in. Anchor rows mark Liquidation, Margin Call, MVRV Floor, Ceiling Target and Cycle ATH.
